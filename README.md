🌱 Meyra AI Agent Plant Health

Human-centered, AI-powered agricultural support for Ethiopian farmers.

Meyra is a digital agricultural agent designed to connect AI technology, agricultural expertise, human support, and farmer realities. It helps farmers make better decisions across the agricultural value chain—from plant health and sustainable production to post-harvest management and market access.

🎯 Our Mission

To make trusted agricultural knowledge and practical support accessible to farmers, while putting people, local knowledge, and real agricultural needs at the center of technology.

🌾 What Meyra Supports

- 🩺 Plant Health — Identification and management of crop diseases, pests, and nutrient deficiencies.
- 🌿 Organic & Sustainable Farming — Practical approaches to environmentally responsible crop production.
- 🐛 Integrated Pest Management — Safer and locally appropriate pest-management recommendations.
- 📦 Post-Harvest Loss Reduction — Guidance to help farmers protect crop quality and reduce avoidable losses.
- 📈 Market Access — Agricultural information and support aimed at improving farmers' participation in markets and value chains.
- 👨‍🌾 Human-Centered Agricultural Extension — AI complements, rather than replaces, farmers, extension workers, agronomists, and local agricultural knowledge.
- 🌍 Local Context — Designed around the realities, languages, crops, and needs of Ethiopian farming communities.

🤖 AI + Human Expertise

Meyra is not designed as AI alone.

The platform combines:

Artificial Intelligence + Agronomic Knowledge + Human Expertise + Local Knowledge + Farmer Experience

AI helps make agricultural information faster and more accessible, while human expertise and farmer realities remain central to responsible decision-making.

🗣️ Designed for Ethiopia

Meyra is being developed with Ethiopian agricultural contexts in mind, including support for:

- Afaan Oromoo
- Amharic
- English

The platform focuses on practical agricultural challenges faced by smallholder farmers and seeks to bridge the gap between knowledge, technology, and action in the field.

🌱 Vision

A future where every farmer can access trusted agricultural knowledge, practical support, sustainable production solutions, and better market opportunities—regardless of location or access to traditional extension services.

Meyra: Technology that serves farmers, not technology that replaces them.
